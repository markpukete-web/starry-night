## What was built

- **A3, one clock.** `DioramaExperience`'s `Scene` owns the sky clock ref; `PaintingFlowSky3D` writes it (its own
  ref stays the fallback for the 2D routes) and `Diorama` hands it to the cypress and the village. The foreground
  reads it one frame late, like the halos; 0 while paused, so the still is the t = 0 state.
- **A4, the cypress flicker** (`src/scene/cypressFlicker.ts`, `BrushCypress.tsx`). Per vertex `aFlicker` = [w, h]:
  `h` the stroke axis (the generator's `heightFraction`; the solid's source v is 1 − h), `w` from the painting's own
  strokes: the tongue term on the stroke's mean |lateral| (0.40–0.75, the shipped strokes' 75th–95th percentiles),
  the tip term on the vertex's height within its OWN lobe (so a frond's tip lights like the main flame's), floor
  0.12, back strokes half the tongue. Per fragment, through `onBeforeCompile` on three's stock `MeshBasicMaterial`:
  `L = 1 + knob · 0.6 · w · env(t) · Σ Aᵢ sin(ωᵢ t − kᵢ h)`, two modes (1.9 / 3.1 s, 1.3 / 0.8 cycles over the tree,
  both climbing at 0.40 tree heights per second), `env` a branch that is exactly 0 at t = 0 and rises over 1.5 s.
  The underpaint solid takes the tip term at half the knob, so the body follows the strokes faintly. "Up" is a
  reading of a flame, declared and tunable.
- **A5, the window glimmer** (`src/scene/villageGlow.ts`, `villageGeometry.ts`, `Village.tsx`). The seven lit marks
  (10 vertices each) carry `aGlow` = [1, ω₁, ω₂], their two rates hashed per window on the CPU (2.3–4.1 s and
  0.9–1.6 s) and packed, `flat`; everything else in the paint mesh 0. `L = 1 + knob · 0.6 · (0.7 sin ω₁t + 0.3 sin
  ω₂t)` per fragment. With the flag on the paint mesh gets its own material; the solid keeps the stock one it used
  to share.
- **Off path.** With the flag off no attribute is packed, no material is touched and three's own programs draw the
  foreground: today's bytes (gate 2). With it on, every new term is exactly 0 at t = 0 (gate 3).
- **Knobs.** `flicker`, `glimmer` 0–1 (`?alive=1&flicker=&glimmer=`), two sliders on `?tune=1`, shipped at 0.5 in
  `sky-tuning.json` (aliveFlicker, aliveGlimmer), the writer's allowlist extended.
- **Tooling and tests.** `scripts/check-cypress-colour.ts` prints the stencil's rejected share (F12);
  `scripts/capture-sky-frames.mjs` self-checks at the first t > 0 too (F16). `scripts/sky-alive.test.ts` T14–T19,
  120 tests green; mutation proofs run and reverted: no tongue term → T14 red, no envelope → T15 red, an off-by-one
  lit range → T16 red.
