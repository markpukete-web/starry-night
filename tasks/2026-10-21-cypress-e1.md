## What was built

   and closed after (317,307 px, 422 × 1578), the main span on every scan row, a field that keeps the painting's

   envelope: the wedge), a root just below each chain's base inside the main.
3. `ebee43f` the ribbons: traced in the field, grown both ways from a seed, draped on the compiled rings' union
   with both edges lifted and the offset along the view, the sky's stroke body as an injection on
   `MeshBasicMaterial` with alpha-to-coverage, the flicker concatenated after it, an optional vertex transform
   for D1, the underpaint `#151c1b` with no map, the flow's failure path to the solid alone. The old generator,
   tracer and tendril tracks deleted with their tests. The gates: the flat ribbon gate, the silhouette gate with
   an x-offset search, the colour gate on each side's own mask with empty-region failure, shared helpers.
4. `0adcf25` the flicker's tongue band on the ribbons' laterals, 0.72–0.93.
5. `066c88d` retune 2: the texel's own pigment, no lift, the stroke body softened at the ribbon's screen width.
6. `0c341a8` retune 3: ribbons at the painting's stroke width (half-widths 0.03–0.06 crop widths, capped at 0.45
   of the seed's run), 450 front, the band's mean pigment, the edges clamped at the rim, the sky's two lanes back.
7. retune 4: the strokes blend their shaped edge (transparent, depth written) instead of alpha-to-coverage, which
   dithered every edge to a stipple at 1:1; one bristle lane (the fine octave aliased at this width too).

## Codex's code review (G12)

Codex (`w4:p4`, read-only, `scratch/cypress-e1-2026-10-21/code-review-report.md`, against `3ae5005`; it raycast the
emitted strips' corners and centroids against the real compiled solid, rendered the two-ribbon overlap case,
corrupted the colour gate's input, ran the derive with fresh decodes, and read the memo in all four branches):
**REVISE**, eight P2s and two P3s. Each was checked against the code and holds; the fixes are one commit after
retune 4, verified as below.

- F1 (P2) a flat strip between two lifted edges cut into the curved lobe (a centroid 0.016 behind the solid) →
  the strip is lifted in five columns across its width (`ribbonColumns`, `drapeRibbon`, `pushDrapedRibbon`);
  the clearance test probes every emitted triangle's corners and centroid and keeps the two-column strip as its
  control (worst 0.003 behind or more, by construction).
- F2 (P2) a transparent material with depth written lost the stroke behind a soft edge drawn first → opaque with
  `alphaTest` 0.5: the flanks are the geometry's edges, which the canvas's MSAA smooths, the rounded head and
  tail a hard cut, order-independent in one unsorted mesh.
- F3 (P2) the silhouette helper unioned every component crossing its column (two fragments moved the tree's top
  by 18 rows) → the largest component on the column.
- F4 (P2) the colour gate selected pixels by the colours under test, so 77% of the tree painted cyan still passed
  → the painting's mask is aligned into the capture (the silhouette gate's transform, shared in
  `scripts/lib/silhouette.ts`) as the expected area, and the share of it the render's treeish component does
  not cover is bounded at 0.35; a recoloured-tree control joins the sky-only one in G7.
- F5 (P2) the width cap compared the unrounded coordinate with integer run endpoints → the rounded texel the
  mask reads, inclusive; tested at a satellite's endpoint and on a one-pixel run.
- F6 (P2) a seed that failed the lift returned the run below it, seedless → null; tested at the crop's top.
- F7 (P2) the continuity test swept no lobe switch and no offset → it bisects a real surface crossing (h 0.35 on
  the shipped plan) and asserts the view offset's jump under 1e-6 where the old normal offset's is 0.0015.
- F8 (P2) the shipped assets did not reproduce from the reviewer's fresh decode (662 seed rows from y 92 against
  the bake's 658 from 96) → on this Mac `sips` is deterministic (three decodes, one md5, equal to the bake's
  cache); the bake now records its decoded inputs' md5s in the row table, so a decoder change shows as a digest
  change rather than a silent shift. The reviewer's decode path is asked for in the confirmation read.
- F9 (P3) the flat gate's row table was typed as `{ uv }` → `RibbonRows`.
- F10 (P3) the overshoot test overclaimed "ends only" while the walk bridges holes → the contract stated (at most
  `overshootSteps` outside samples on each side of the seed, anywhere) and tested with a bridged hole.
