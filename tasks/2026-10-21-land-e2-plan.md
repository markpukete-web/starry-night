## What the painting's land is (the 1:1 crops)

- **The fields** under and around the village (v 0.78–1.0): dark blue-green and navy ground in long, mostly flat
  strokes, 15–30 px wide at 3840, 100–400 px long; a band of dark teal foliage with pale curling strokes
  behind the houses; the houses' walls stand on it.
- **The hills** (v 0.60–0.78): blue-violet rolling masses in diagonal strokes leaning 40–60° from vertical, the
  big hill at the right, the foot dissolving into the fields.
- **The olive trees**: rounded masses of curling teal and blue strokes, each a cluster of arcs 150–250 px wide
  (0.04–0.06 of the width), in two rows along the right hill's foot (u 0.74–1.0, v 0.70–0.82) and singly among
  the houses (behind the church, left of the striped roof); dark green-blue with pale blue highlights.
- **The bushes** at the cypress's foot (u 0.02–0.2, v 0.86–1.0): yellow-green curls outlined in blue, 100–200 px
  blobs, on the ground in front of the trunk.

## Design

The principle from the review: the identity objects are authored (the masses, their placements, the exporter's
closure policy), the fill is procedural (the ground's strokes, the masses' curls), and every claim the gate
makes is measured in the metric the code uses.

### E2.1 — the derivation (`scripts/derive-land.ts`, `scripts/lib/cypress-field.ts`, `scripts/lib/land-field.ts`)

`blendToVertical` takes the fallback (`VERTICAL`, `HORIZONTAL`); `landClass` names the band's paint. Both are
built and tested (`1181e3b`).

The band v ∈ [0.58, 1.0] of the 3840 scan at a quarter of the resolution, **960 × 319** (3 × 3 blocks were 778 KB
encoded; the quarter with the anisotropy quantised to 32 levels measured 397 KB against a 500 KB bar, and the
pixel claims below are in that frame). The field: `orientationField(σ 1.5)`, `lowPassOrientation(σ 1.5)`,
`blendToVertical(…, HORIZONTAL)`. One asset (confirmation read, F6): **`land-flow.png`** carries the orientation
(R, G, undirected: the double-angle resolved to dx ≥ 0) over the **whole band, the cypress column included**
(the bushes' box lies under the column and the masses read the tensor there), and in B the ground's admission
with the anisotropy: `max(8, anisotropy × 31 × 8)` where the texel's painting uv maps through `worldAt` (E2.2)
inside the coast shrunk by the widest ribbon's reach (`coastR × 0.97`), 0 elsewhere. The ground tracer seeds on
B; each mass crops R, G and sets B 255 on its own runs, so no mass is starved by the ground's admission. **The
cypress column's fill is a run-time rule** (u < 0.26, v < 0.95; `landStrokes.ts`): the painting has no ground
there and the filled painting does not fill it below the sky band, so the ground's walk reads a copy of the flow
with the column's R, G set horizontal, and a ribbon seeded in the column takes the pigment of the texel at u 0.26
on its own v; the terrain under the column (x −2.05 to −1.47, visible from the orbit) is clad like the rest. The
overlay dims the unsupported texels and hatches the column.

### E2.3 — the ground strokes (`src/scene/landStrokes.ts`, `BrushIsland.tsx`, `cypressStrokes.ts`)

**The tracer gains a continuous heading** (F9): `traceCypressRibbons` takes `config.heading: 'up' | 'continuous'`.
`'up'` is the cypress's walk as built (the field is signed up at bake time). `'continuous'` flips a texel's
direction when it opposes the walk's last step (the sky's rule, `streamlineGeometry.ts`), so a horizontal lane
whose neighbouring texels decode to opposites walks on; and a walk ends when its new point is within a quarter
step of the point two steps back (a two-step cycle). The land bakes its field undirected for this walk (the
double-angle resolved to the `dx ≥ 0` half-plane); the cypress's asset and route are untouched.

`LAND_RIBBON_CONFIG` (fractions of the 960 × 319 flow): `frontCount` 1,000, `backShare` 0, lengths 0.1–0.3 of
the height (32–96 px, the painting's 130–380 at 3840), `step` 0.0094 (3 px), **half-widths 0.002–0.004 of the
width** (1.9–3.8 px at 960: full strokes 15–31 scan pixels, the measured 15–30; confirmation read, F8),
`overshootSteps` 0, `taper` 0.7, `heading: 'continuous'`, the pigment the plain band mean (`accept` everything).

The drape (`drapeOnTerrain`, built 2026-10-21) maps the ribbon's **centreline** through `flowToPainting` and
`worldAt` and lays the width across it **in world**, `halfWidth × worldPerU` tapered to the tip, so a stroke is
the same width whatever the map's stretch along z (the first design built the columns in the crop and a
horizontal stroke's width followed the hills' 8–18 world per v; F8's two formulas are superseded by one:
`halfWidth × 2 × 4.75`, isotropic). Every column point is lifted along the terrain's normal at its own (x, z) by
`LAND_LIFT` 0.02, and the metric the test uses is the same: `(y − islandHeightAt(x, z)) × normal.y` within
[0.5, 1.5] × `LAND_LIFT`, the final (x, z) inside the coast (F7). The seed domain is the flow's mask; a seed at
the mask's edge that can walk nowhere is dropped by the tracer and the deficit is traced again with the next
seed (three rounds), so the count is the count; the test asserts ≥ 980 of 1,000 traced and draped on the shipped
asset. The widths are measured on the drape for twenty horizontal and five vertical ribbons on the apron, both
within [0.6, 1.4] × the one expected width. Degenerate strips: no step's two outer columns under 0.004.

**The cypress column** (`CYPRESS_COLUMN`, `columnSource`): the tree's silhouette in the band traced per v
(u 0.15–0.42 at the crown, 0.22–0.29 at the trunk's base); a column texel reads the band translated past the
tree's right edge plus 0.02, and in the hills band at the same depth between the fields' top and the crest at the
source's u (the crest is lower there, so the same v would be sky); the ribbon's pigment reads the same source.
A horizontal fill with one pigment per row read as planks, and a 0.26-wide rectangle left the crown's flame tips
seeding the tree's brown onto the ground (flat gates 1–3).

**The village's footprint** (`VILLAGE_FOOTPRINT`, seven boxes in painting pixels): the ground's support excludes
it, so no wall, roof or window seeds a ground stroke (the striped roof's sienna and the lit windows' yellow
walked onto the apron; a colour rule could not tell a pale wall from a lit hill slope). The pigment window still
filters by colour (`groundTexel`: no `other` texel that is not green-dominant).

**Two low-passes, one asset**: σ 1.5 on the masses' foliage texels (their curls, dilated by 2), σ 3.5 elsewhere,
since the painting's fields run straight where the fine field curled around every house (flat gate 2).

`BrushIsland`: the top's dabs go, the solid's `topBase` darkens to `PALETTE.hills × 0.3` in linear light (≈ 0.58
in sRGB, L 28, under the darkest strokes; 0.55 linear sat at L 36, lighter than a quarter of the strokes, and the
apron flattened, capture 1), and **the root keeps its stream** (F14): the root's `mulberry32` is advanced past the

positions before and after the change (the hash is banked in the test from `main`'s build). The ground strokes

### E2.4 — the trees and bushes (`src/scene/landMasses.ts`, `landDomes.ts`, `LandTrees.tsx`)

A mass is a row of domes along its authored base: `count = max(1, round(width / (ry × 1.4)))`, each dome's
centre x evenly along the base, `rx = 0.75 × width / count`, `ry = height / 2` with `height = boxHeight_px ×
0.0038` (the houses' scale; the rows come out 0.16–0.53, the orchard 0.35–0.58, within a house's height, F5),
`rz = min(rx, ry) × 0.5`. **The dome's base is the terrain** (F5): `centre.y = islandHeightAt(cx, z) + ry`, so the
ellipsoid's lowest point is on the ground.

**One x per source column, the frontmost dome** (F4): a source point `(s, t)` of the mass's box maps to `x = x0 +
s × width` and `y = base + (1 − t) × height`; every dome whose ellipse contains that `(x, y)` gives a depth, the
largest wins (E1's union), and beyond every dome the nearest rim's plane within 0.6 of a radius, else null. The
position is continuous across dome boundaries by construction; the test sweeps `s` across each boundary of
`row-back` in steps of 1e-4 and asserts no step over 0.002.

```ts
export function liftToDomes(domes: Dome[], mass: Placed, s: number, t: number, front: boolean): { position: Vector3; lateral: number } | null {
  const x = mass.x - mass.width / 2 + s * mass.width
  const y = mass.base + (1 - t) * mass.height
  const facing = front ? 1 : -1
  let best: { position: Vector3; lateral: number } | null = null
  let bestDepth = -Infinity
  let nearest: { dome: Dome; r: number } | null = null
  for (const dome of domes) {
    const ax = (x - dome.centre.x) / dome.rx
    const ay = (y - dome.centre.y) / dome.ry
    const r = Math.hypot(ax, ay)
    if (r <= 1) {
      const depth = Math.sqrt(1 - r * r) * dome.rz
      if (depth > bestDepth) { bestDepth = depth; best = { position: new Vector3(x, y, dome.centre.z + facing * depth), lateral: r } }
    } else if (!nearest || r - 1 < nearest.r - 1) nearest = { dome, r }
  }
  if (best) return best
  if (!nearest || nearest.r - 1 > 0.6) return null
  return { position: new Vector3(x, y, nearest.dome.centre.z), lateral: nearest.r }
}
```

The strip's lift is **along the facing axis** (+z for the front, −z for the back; confirmation read, F4: a lift
along the winning ellipsoid's normal jumped 0.0096 where the union switches domes, as E1's normal offset did at
its lobes), 0.012; the test drapes a ribbon through `row-back`'s crossings and asserts the emitted columns' steps
under 0.002, with the normal offset as the control that fails. **The contact policy** (F5): any emitted point
below `islandHeightAt(x, z) + 0.005` is raised to that height, so no strip enters the ground; the test checks the
emitted, lifted columns, not the centre.

The cladding per mass: the flow's crop over the mass's box (the band's resolution), B kept on the mass's own
foliage pixels from the JSON's runs and 0 elsewhere (the ground's support plays no part); `MASS_RIBBON_CONFIG`:
count `clamp(pixels / 40, 24, 180)` with `pixels` the mass's own-mask area at the quarter resolution
(confirmation read, F16: the two smaller orchard boxes cannot reach 60; the bar is ≥ 24 for every mass and ≥ 60
for `orchard-upper`), lengths 0.2–0.5 of
the box's height, step 2 px of the box, half-widths 0.04–0.08 of the box's width (the arcs 15–30 px of a 150–250
px mass), `overshootSteps` 3, taper 0.5, `heading: 'continuous'`, the pigment the band mean over the mass's class
pixels only. The back face takes 0.4 of the count. Each dome has an underpaint solid (an 8 × 6 ellipsoid, the
class's darkest texel colour in the box) at `renderOrder` 2, the strips at 3.

The trees are built in `BrushIsland`'s second memo beside the ground (one load of the painting and the flow, one
ready ref), not in a `LandTrees.tsx` of their own; the baked masses come from `land-masses.json` by fetch.

**D1's contract** (F10): the island's group exposes two named meshes, `land-trees-solid` (every dome's underpaint, one
geometry) and `land-trees-strokes`; D1 bends both with one position-based field weighted by the vertex's height
within its dome (the solid's vertices carry it from the ellipsoid's `up`, the strips' from `1 − t`), so the
paint and the body move together; `land-trees-solid` is D1's raycast target in place of `village-foliage`; the
ground takes no bend. D1's plan is revised to this when D1 starts.
