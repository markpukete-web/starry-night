## What was built

- **A screen point to a painting point** (`dioramaSkyProjection.ts`: `uvFromSkyDirection`, `skyPointFromRay`,
  `homeInverseQuaternion`; `useTouchInput.ts`). The ray through the pointer meets the dome's FAR root, whether the
  camera is inside the dome or outside it at the orbit's extremes, is rotated into the sky's frame by the inverse
  of `camera × homeInverse`, and inverted analytically. Listeners on the element R3F connects events to, `up` and
  `cancel` on `window`; drei's OrbitControls coexists, so on touch a one-finger drag both orbits and trails.
- **The surge** (`touchSurge.ts`, both ribbon shaders under a `TOUCH` define on A's variant). Per vertex `aTouch` =
  [u, v, tx, ty] (the side pair's mean and the ribbon's unit tangent, isotropic metric) and per ribbon `aSeg` (its
  centreline segment), packed only with the flag. The LIFT per vertex: a pulse sent downstream along the tangent
  (speed 0.25 of the canvas width per second, σ 0.06 along, 0.025 across) and a bloom along the stroke (σ 0.08 ×
  0.04), a rise of 0.08 s, decay τ 1.6 s, weighted by the ribbon's gust weight and capped at A's ceiling 0.5; up
  to 12 live, 16 slots, the oldest fading over 0.25 s on eviction. The KICK per ribbon from its segment (σ 0.05):
  0.4 s of clock at full amplitude, rising over 0.4 s, decaying over 3 s, closed to exactly zero by 10 s and only
  then pruned, never evicted live (max 4); the clock never steps back. Every falloff is cut to exactly zero beyond
  3σ, so an untouched ribbon is untouched in bytes. Both terms sit under count guards after A's statements, so
  with no input A's path runs verbatim.
- **Gestures.** Mouse hover and drag trail lift-only surges (amp 0.5, at most one per 60 ms and 0.02 u); a tap
  (≤ 300 ms, ≤ 8 px) sends a strong surge (amp 1) with a kick, and flares a star or the moon it lands on (0.04 /
  0.06 isotropic units; `skyTargets.ts`, the moon at its painted centre). A flare multiplies the halo's colour
  (opacity clips under additive blending) by up to 2.2 and its scale by up to 1.5, a 0.12 s rise and a 1.4 s fall.
- **Store, seams, knob.** `Scene` owns the store; the sky's frame loop prunes it and writes the uniform arrays in
  place; cleared on pause, reduced motion and the material rebuild. Seams with the flag: `window.__touchState()`,
  `__touchInject(u, v, amp, t0, kick)`, `__camera()`. Knob `surge` (0–1.5, shipped 1) on `?tune=1`, whose share
  URL keeps `touch=1`; `sky-tuning.json` `touchSurge`.
- **Tests.** `scripts/touch-surge.test.ts` (T22, T22b, T22c, T23, T25) and T20, T21, T24 in `sky-alive.test.ts`:
  128 green. Mutation checks during the build: the anisotropy assertion, the support edges, the kick's zero at
  the window, the live cap and the refusal each failed when the term they guard was broken.

Two findings of the gate itself, fixed before the table above: (a) a Gaussian never reaches zero, so the first
model moved every ribbon by invisible amounts and the twin's mask covered the sky (`65a4e46`: finite support);
(b) under software rendering a frame takes most of a second, so DevTools' awaited press-then-release landed a
frame apart and every synthetic tap read as a drag; a probe showed the two 972 ms apart with a 60 ms sleep and
1 ms apart when fired together, which is how the tool now sends them. The code was right; the tool was not.
