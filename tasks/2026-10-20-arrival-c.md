# C — arrival: record and gate, 2026-10-20

Slice C of "Direction — make it alive": the opening. Plan `tasks/2026-10-20-arrival-c-plan.md` (reviewed by Codex
before the build, REVISE, ten findings applied). Built on the Mac on `feat/arrival-c` from `docs/after-pr-11`
(= `main` `5bbe243` plus the record), behind `?arrival=1`. The flag implies nothing: `?arrival=1&alive=1&touch=1`
opens into everything. With the flag off, every route is byte-identical to `main` (G3). Not pushed: Mark's feel on
the local preview first (`http://127.0.0.1:5180/?arrival=1`, the production build), then Codex's code review,
then the push and the PR.

## What was built

- `src/scene/arrival.ts` (`3e87d9a`): the stage constants (`ARRIVAL`, logged in Tunables), `arrivalFrame(a)`,
  the camera on the home's axis (`arrivalCamera`, the home tuple itself at the end), and `ArrivalClock` (waits for
  the scene, bounds a frame to 0.1 s, skips at 8×, opens anyway after 15 s, latches). Nine node tests, red on the
  missing module first.
- The opening (`55cd955`): `DioramaExperience.tsx` owns the state, the refs and the DOM layer (the scan, Vincent's
  line, the citation); `ArrivalDriver` at priority −2 runs the clock and writes the camera, the sky's step and the
  layer's styles per frame; the controls are `enabled={!arriving}`, B's touch off until done; the title and the dock
  `visibility: hidden` until done and fading in over 600 ms. `PaintingFlowSky3D` takes `arrivalStep` (the clock's
  advance while arriving, `dt` otherwise) and a `ready` seam; `BrushCypress` a `ready` seam of its own (its CPU image
  does not suspend). `App.tsx` reads the flag. Reduced motion: the end state from the first frame, the line a static
  caption with its own listeners, gone on the first pointer or key but Tab.
- The capture tool (`26cfe3d`): `--arrival-times` (both pins in one expression, the first pair installed before
  navigation, validated non-decreasing, file names carry the arrival time) and `--screenshot` (the layer is DOM).

The sequence: hold 4 s (the scan on a dark ground, the line beneath), the line fades over the hold's last second,
the dissolve 1.5 s (the ground over its first half, then the scan, which grows to 1.12), the sky's clock eased
0 → 1 over 3 s and the camera pushed from distance 5.55 to the home over 4 s, both from 0.75 s into the dissolve,
done at 8.75 s. The line: letter 777 to Theo, the French public domain, the English this project's own rendering.
