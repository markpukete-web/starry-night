## Task 2: the layout, the fold and the direction

**The fold's cause is the facets.** Evidence (`t2-fold-probe.txt`, `t2/seg-compare-4x.png`):

- The inner edge crossing itself: 2.0% of the blades at stop 0 and 0.4% at stop 2, all small far ones (16 px tall at
  most). The near arches 200 px tall, where the V shows at 2×, never reverse.
- The facets: three segments turn `|curl| / 3` at each joint, 24.5° at the median and up to 38°. The same pose with six
  segments (19° at most) turns every V into a curve at 4×.
- The clamp touches only the root's edge. The yaw is at most 29° off facing, a 13% squeeze, not edge-on. The discards
  shape the tip and do not bend the blade.

**The fix.** Four segments, and no joint turns more than `jointMax` 0.38 rad (22°): a blade's curl is capped at 1.52.
The cap mostly trims the blades that curl against their lean. The outer blades still stop at `tipMax` 1.8 and fall over.
After it (`t2-fold-probe-after.txt`): the joints' median turn is 18.5°. The inner edge now crosses on 3.0% of the
blades at stop 0, at 1.2–2.4 from the camera, 10–16 px tall. That is open for the 2× sheets of Task 3.

**The budget.** Four segments cost a third more triangles. Measured at the walk's worst point (`t2-budget-*.txt`):

- A chunk draws every blade when any of it is in view, and 46% of the drawn blades were off screen. Halving the chunks'
  length (`GRASS.dt` 0.05 → 0.025) took three segments from 100.0% of the budget to 85.2%. The visible chunks went from
  at most 122 to at most 231, so the draw calls nearly double. Task 4's perf measures that.
- Splitting the outer band did not help once every band was built. `grassChunks` looped over three bands by a
  hard-coded count, and a probe with four bands silently dropped the outermost one. The loop now follows `GRASS.bands`.
- More segments only for the near chunks cost 72–98% over the budget: the near chunks hold most of the triangles.
- The layout changed with the clump's new draws, and the refined worst point sits high: 104.1% at density 0.18, 98.0%
  at 0.17 (8 over the 2% margin), 92.3% at 0.165. The density is 0.165, 18,500 blades against slice 3's 22,842.

**The direction.** A clump's blades curl to `clumpSide(x, z)`, a value-noise field at 5 per world unit, and 15% of
them (`against`) curl the other way. The tests: at least 75% curl with the side where main's fountain gave half, and
two clumps 0.03 apart agree at least 80% of the time.

**The paint.** `seed` is drawn first, and the pigment and top colour come from `mulberry32(seed)`. Two paintings that
differ in every texel give one layout. So the budget test now runs on a synthetic painting, in CI as well.

**The look after Task 2** (`t2/look/`): the median L is 23.2–25.9 (the painting 22.2, Task 0 16.3–17.6). The grass's
ΔE is 1.0–1.7. The near arches are curves at 2×. About half the blades now read upright: a blade that leans one way and
curls the other turns back to the vertical. The painting's strokes lie over in low arcs. Open for Task 3, question 1.

**Not built:** a per-frame cap on the grass's triangles would hold the ceiling at every pose, not at sampled ones, and
free the density. It changes the frame loop and the plan, so it is noted for Mark, not built.
