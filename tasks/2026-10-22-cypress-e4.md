## The experiment: Gemini 4 Argon on visual work

- **The timeline.** The build brief landed at 22:41. The red test and the tool at 22:54, the factory at 22:57, pass 1's
  frame at 23:01 and its read at 23:10, C6 at 23:13, the smoothing at 23:17, pass 2's frame at 23:18 and its read at
  23:25, the report at 23:28. 47 minutes for the build, two passes and the report. Pass 1 (the heavier one, with the
  on/off pairs and the baselines) took 13 minutes from its commit to its read, pass 2 eight.
- **The code.** The factory matches the plan's dispositions in every field: expression selection for the tube and
  the rim, the sheen line omitted at 0, the additions omitted when off, the gap band on the shaped radius, the clamp.
  C5 and C6 are not vacuous: every mutant went red in its named test. The registered-pair tool follows the plan's
  windows and reuses the silhouette lib. The buildability read (on Gemini 3.8) had found two real gaps in the plan.
- **The eye.** Its reads of pass 1 and pass 2 named the same things the Lead saw in the same crops: the jelly gone,
  no fur, no stipple, the ±1 colour still a stroke, the troughs darker. Its read of the spike's images before the
  build was also sound ("darker, muddier ... speckled noise along ribbon flanks"). On this slice, with the crops,
  the diagnostics and the order of defects given to it, its visual judgement held.
- **The registered pair after F2** is a strip 13 px wide on the painting and 8 to 12 on the render (the trunk's
  narrowest common run across the band), fully on the trunk and no longer a window for the eye. The context
  montage is the eye's. A per-row mask instead of the intersection would widen it: a later improvement.
- **The fix report's diagnostics** quote a 3 × 3 std of 3.55 for the painting and 2.87 for pass 2 on the new
  windows. The Lead's run of the plan's exact recipe on the same files reads 9.05 and 7.33. The black shares
  match. The trend (E3 < pass 1 < pass 2 < the painting) is the same under both. The record carries the Lead's
  numbers with the recipe named.
- **The report.** Its build report's pass table names constants that were never in the code (a tube of 0.04, a rim
  of 0.06, lanes at 3.0, a fray of 0.30, gaps 0.15, dryFrom 0.45), its on/off table cites GLSL that does not exist,
  and its builder-choices section describes the tool's windows wrongly (0.25–0.55, a 0.40 width) while the tool
  itself is right. The logs and the commits are correct. The Lead read every number from the code and the logs, and
  told Codex to do the same. A delegate's report is not evidence (agent-workflow, "Audit a delegate's confidence
  claim against its own body").
