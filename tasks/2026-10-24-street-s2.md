  the first second, and a fling ends at 0.8999999999975, not 0.9. Fixed: keys and the automatic walk are velocities that

  stop 4.
- **F3, a stop leaves queued motion.** True: blur after a second of keys left 0.0356 of backlog, 0.0128 of it walked in

- **F4, the reference walk fails the 0.004 step bound.** True: 0.00441 at `t` 0.331. The Lead's first reading was

- **F5, the construction does not fit the box, and the projection tests a proxy.** True: the crown topped out at 0.081,
  and the hem alone is 0.0372 wide against a 0.035 bound. Fixed: the crown reaches 0.085. The rig carries the body box

  The canvas moves to `−x`.
- **F6, the tap has no pointer ownership or cancel.** True. Fixed: one primary pointer owns a gesture, its `pointerId`
