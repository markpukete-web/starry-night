## Plan review, round 1 (2026-10-24, afternoon)

### Codex (`w4:p4`): REVISE, eight P2 findings, all accepted and folded into revision 2

The Lead checked each one against the code at `8b941f9` before folding it.

- **F1, a new `STREET_SOURCES` key breaks two exhaustive tables.** True: `SOURCE_STROKES` (`streetStrokes.ts:65`) and
  `SOURCE_CLASSES` (`streetGeometry.ts:68`) are `Record<StreetSourceName, …>`. Fixed differently from the suggestion:
  Vincent's sources are his own (`VINCENT_SOURCES` in `vincent.ts`), and `sourceUnderpaint`'s body moves to
  `rectUnderpaint(painting, rect, share)`. His strokes are not traced faces, so a dummy stroke config would be wrong.
- **F2, the easing cannot meet the speed tests.** True: the plan's recurrence gives 76% of the key and auto speeds in
  the first second, and a fling ends at 0.8999999999975, not 0.9. Fixed: keys and the automatic walk are velocities that
  move `t` and `target` together. Only the wheel eases. A snap within 1e-4 world units, and the automatic walk ends at
  stop 4.
- **F3, a stop leaves queued motion.** True: blur after a second of keys left 0.0356 of backlog, 0.0128 of it walked in
  the next 0.5 s. Fixed: `stopWalk` (target = t) on blur, a hidden page, the pause, reduced motion turning on, and a
  tap that stops him. The tests first build a backlog, then stop.
- **F4, the reference walk fails the 0.004 step bound.** True: 0.00441 at `t` 0.331. The Lead's first reading was
  wrong: "the camera swings wider on a bend", with the bound raised to 0.005. The worker found the cause (W3, below):
  the lane has two kinks. Fixed at the cause, and the bound stays 0.004.
- **F5, the construction does not fit the box, and the projection tests a proxy.** True: the crown topped out at 0.081,
  and the hem alone is 0.0372 wide against a 0.035 bound. Fixed: the crown reaches 0.085. The rig carries the body box
  (half-width 0.025, half-depth 0.02, the shadow left out). `vincentBoxCorners` feeds the frame test, the door test and
  `__vincentBox`. The build test reads its bounds from the rig. The Lead re-ran the door check at that box
  (`probe-f5.ts`): clear by 0.063 on a desktop and 0.132 on a phone. The frame sweep (`probe-f5-frame.ts`): the box
  reaches |x| 0.993 on the phone at one bend. That is inside the frame, so it goes to the look gate. While folding F5
  the Lead found a defect of its own: the table put `+x` as his right while he faces `+z`. In three that is his left.
  The canvas moves to `−x`.
- **F6, the tap has no pointer ownership or cancel.** True. Fixed: one primary pointer owns a gesture, its `pointerId`
  must match on the up, and `pointercancel`, blur and a hidden page drop it. `classifyGesture` and `TOUCH_SURGE`'s
  300 ms and 8 px are reused, and the plan's own `isTap` and constants are removed. The browser check adds a second
  touch and a cancelled touch.
- **F7, the rotation check passes after a reset.** True. Fixed: `walkMode=input`, assert `t` is 0.55 before the turn
  and after it.
- **F8, the walk overwrites the flat panel's camera.** True: `Street.tsx:139–159`. Fixed: `walkOn(flags)` is false
  for `flat`, so no walk, no Vincent and no listener there. The browser check asserts `FLAT_VIEW`'s camera.

### The worker (`w4:p5`): GAPS, twelve gaps, all answered in revision 2

- **W1, no 1440 × 900 viewport.** The capture scripts' desktop is 1600 × 900. Captures use the scripts' viewports. The
  unit tests keep aspect 1.6, which is stricter on |x|.
- **W2, Task 0's commands and which perf configuration.** Written out: `capture-sky-frames.mjs` per viewport and
  station, two runs compared with `cmp`, and `desktop-final` and `mobile-viewport-final` read from each perf run.
- **W3, the lane's kinks (the cause of F4).** The worker traced the 0.00441 step to the join of the lane's first two
  Béziers, where the tangent changes in one step. The Lead's scan (`probe-joins3.ts`) found a second at 0.684.
  `streetLayout.ts:26–28`: c1 leaves along (20, −120) and c2 starts along (20, −110). c2 leaves along (10, −120) and
  c3 starts along (10, −100). Each kink turns the tangent about 0.85°, so the camera jumps about 0.0025 sideways in
  one frame. The walk rounds them with `walkTangent`, which leans toward the secant inside 0.02 of a join. The camera's
  second difference falls from 0.00307 to 0.00004 (0.00003 away from the joins), and its largest step to 0.00292. The
  stations are unchanged, because the nearest one is 0.024 from a join. The lane itself is not changed, because S1b's
  houses, strokes and stations sit on it. A test pins the rounding.
- **W4, Task 1's details.** `walkPose` and `stationPose` take `portrait`. `walkPose` returns `near` and `far`. A local
  `smoothstep` in GLSL's argument order (three's takes `x` first). `vincentPlace`'s formulas are written out.
- **W5, the gait's waveforms.** Written out: `φ = π × distance / stride`, lift `|sin φ|`, roll `sin φ`, arm `cos φ`.
- **W6, the arm's swing.** The left arm is its own geometry in a child group at its shoulder, rotated, so no vertex
  is rewritten and the underpaint swings with the strokes.
- **W7, the moon's light in a basic material.** `vincentMoon(lit)` is written out: the world normal from
  `mat3(modelMatrix) * normal`, flipped by `gl_FrontFacing`, constants as literals, appended to the stroke body so it
  runs before the fog. Without that order the fog's colour would have been darkened too.
- **W8, the camera before the first frame.** The layout effect places it at `walkPose(initialT)` on mount.
- **W9, the colour gate's commands and minimum areas.** Written out: smock 800 px and hat 80 px. The Lead estimates
  5,000 and 500 on the desktop, 1,600 and 150 on the phone.
- **W10, the phone's perf configuration walks away.** Every perf URL carries `walkMode=input`.
- **W11, no clip script.** The clip moves to the Lead's Task 6, recorded with the Playwright browser.
- **W12, Task 5's commit scope.** Both scripts in one commit.
