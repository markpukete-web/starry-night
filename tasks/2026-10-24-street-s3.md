## Task 1, the blade

**What the painting shows.** The bushes at the lower right (1445–1600 × 1155–1267) are arching strokes. Each rises
from the clump, bends over and falls outward. Measured at 8×, a stroke is 60–70 px long and 5–8 px wide at 1:1, so
8–11 times as long as it is wide, and keeps its width until a short tip. They are blue-black, green and yellow-olive,
dark at a clump's centre and pale at its edge. The grass test's blade was 5.5 times as long as wide, so the width was
not the fault Mark named. The test's blades were short, straight, tapered all along and one even grey-green.

**The model** (`src/scene/grassBlade.ts`). A blade is a ribbon in a vertical plane that faces the camera, as a
brushstroke faces its viewer, turned off it by `yaw` (± 0.5 rad). Its angle from the vertical grows from `lean` at the
root by `curl` at the tip, and its spine is the closed-form integral of that angle, so a tip can pass the horizontal
as the painting's do, and never bends past 1.9 rad. `bladeVertex` is the one formula: the flat strip bakes it, the
reach measures it, and `GRASS_BLADE_GLSL` is the same function for Task 4's shader. A clump fans its blades from
upright at its centre to `fan` 0.75 rad at its edge, each arching outward. Each blade's colour is one texel of the
field strip or the bushes: the inner blades from the dark end of six picks, the outer from the pale end.

**The reach** (`scratch/s3/probe-blade-reach.ts` in the Lead's worktree). Up to 0.060 at the plan's clump height and
0.076 at the tuft rule's tallest, with the push (0.03) and the wind (0.0025). The plan's probe padded each chunk by
0.04. Task 2's budget test reads `bladeReach`, so the larger spheres show there, and `density` is Task 2's tunable.
The tallest point is 0.0455, under `GRASS.top` 0.05.

**Tests** (`scripts/street-grass-blade.test.ts`, six). Each was proved by a mutant that turns it red: the dark and
pale ends swapped, the tip's cap removed, the push left out of the reach, the widening left out of the reach.
