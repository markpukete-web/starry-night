## Deviations from the plan, for the review

- The strips' width is laid out in world across the mapped centreline (F8's two formulas superseded by one).
- The cypress column is the tree's silhouette per v, filled from the band translated and crest-aligned.
- The ground's support excludes the village's authored footprint (`VILLAGE_FOOTPRINT`).
- The hills band maps to the crest per u, not a flat 0.60.
- Two low-passes in one asset; `land-support.png` was never made (B carries the admission).
- The trees are built in `BrushIsland` beside the ground (named meshes `land-trees-solid`, `land-trees-strokes`
  for D1), not in a `LandTrees.tsx`; the baked masses are fetched.
- Domes capped at 0.3 high; the box's curls compress onto them.
- The strips' frame on a mass rises from the terrain under each point's x, not from the mass centre's base, so the
  strips meet the domes where they stand on a slope (`dd260b0`); inside one dome the frame still slopes against
  the rigid ellipsoid by the terrain's change across that dome's width, under 0.2 heights on orchard-upper.
- 2,000 ribbons, three columns, step 4 px; `topBase` hills × 0.3 linear (the plan said 0.55).
- `landClass` moved to `src/scene/landClass.ts` (the runtime's pigment filter needs it).
