### The passes (`t5/first`, `p1` to `p4`, `p4z`)

The first build read as a painted street at once: houses either side in bold strokes with ink outlines, the steeple in
fog ahead, the painting's sky over the roofs. Named, then changed:

- Pass 1: an alley (fronts 0.1 from the centreline) → 0.2. Big curled swooshes on the near walls → the blend toward the
  wall's axis 0.5 → 0.75 (the walls' cover 3 → 3.5 to close the gaps straighter strokes leave; the cover test read
  0.846 against 0.85). Sparse roofs → cover 6. The trees in E4's body. Flat blue doors → dark.
- Pass 2: the path's pigment through a paler-texel filter left 70% of samples to copy a neighbour → every ground texel,
  lifted 0.35 toward `hillsCrest`. The church's tree against the camera at station 4 → 0.7 from the path. Smaller,
  dimmer lit windows.
- Pass 3: an unpainted navy band at the horizon at stations 2 and 3 (the ground ended 0.75 past the path) → the ground
  to 6 units in larger far tiles with larger strokes, the fog thinned (0.6–1.6 → 0.8–3.5). The band went.
- Pass 4: rectangular blocks on the near path → a calmer body for the ground and the path (no gap discards, soft lanes,
  dry brush from 0.85, half the fray). The blocks stayed. The retune cap (4) is reached.

A diagnostic (not shipped, reverted): the path's colour smoothed over ±8 samples. The blocks stayed: not the colour.

**The cause, and a bug fix, not a pass (`734903f`):** z-fighting. Every ribbon of a face shared one lift, so overlapping
coplanar ribbons fought in the depth buffer close to the camera at a grazing angle. Each ribbon now rides one of 16
levels 0.15 mm apart (resolved to about 3 units with the near plane at 0.004); the outlines and the windows above the
top level. The path became clean long strokes with their bristle grooves; the walls lost their pixel artefacts too.
Pass 4's ground body was aimed at a misdiagnosis. It stays as built because the cap is reached; whether the ground
returns to E4's own body is Mark's call at the gate.

## G5, the sky's method (`t6/`, 16 frames)

A or B (the dome at the origin, the street placed inside it; or the dome following the camera), lock or world (the sky
turning with the camera; or the painting's arc fixed in the world), stations 0 and 4, desktop and portrait. Station 0
passed in all four. At station 4 (tilted 26° to the church's gable) every method showed an unpainted band: A-lock,
A-world and B-world at the top in portrait, B-lock below the painted band at the sides on desktop (the design's C2: under
the lock the camera must not tilt). The cause is the tilt. Chosen: **B and world** (no growth as the camera walks, by
construction; a real sky a turning walk will need), with every station's tilt plus half its vertical field of view at
most 50° (a test pins it; station 4's target from 0.25 to 0.13, a tilt of 10°: 47.5° in portrait). The recapture
(`t6/chosen/`): stations 0, 2 and 4 painted to every edge, desktop and portrait. The sky reads larger than the
diorama's (the camera at the dome's centre). A and lock stay as dev parameters.
