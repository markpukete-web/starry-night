## What was built

- **A1, the tempo.** `src/scene/skyAlive.ts`: each ribbon's gust weight from the painting (band luminance
  0.38–0.52, the whorls' cores, floor 0.15) and its lag (midpoint u × 3 s, left to right). Both ribbon vertex
  shaders compute the warped clock `T(t) = t + w (G(t − lag) − G(−lag))`, periods 9.7 / 5.9 / 16.3 s, amplitudes
  0.36 / 0.22 / 0.12 (tempo 0.3×–1.7× in the band, mean 1), and a brightness lift of up to 6% × w on a surge. The
  light sky's shimmer takes half the warp (the boil knob). Attribute `aGust` packed in `ribbonsToArrays`.
- **A2, the halos.** `SourceOrbs` reads the sky clock through a ref (pinned, advanced, or 0 while paused). Each
  star halo breathes at its own period (4.5–9 s from a hash of its index): opacity ±14%, scale ±5%. The moon's
  halo pulses at 13 s, ±8%; its point light stays steady. No phase offsets, so the t = 0 frame and the paused
  still equal today's; with the flag off every write is the base value.
- **Tooling.** `scripts/capture-sky-frames.mjs` (pinned-clock frames), `scripts/compare-frames.mjs`. Tests
  T7–T11 in `scripts/sky-alive.test.ts`, with mutation proofs (per-vertex weight, whorl term dropped,
  amplitudes summing to 1.2: each turns its test red; the first T7 fixture let the per-vertex mutation pass
  until the ribbon's colours straddled the band).

| Gust amplitudes (sum) | 0.36 / 0.22 / 0.12 (0.70) | 0.46 / 0.28 / 0.16 (0.90) | tempo 0.1×–1.9× in the band instead of 0.3×–1.7× |

| Brightness lift | 0.06 | 0.15 | the surge lights the band |
| Halo breath | ±14% opacity, ±5% scale | ±30%, ±12% | stars noticeably breathe |
| Moon pulse | ±8% | ±18% | the glow swells |

**Live knobs** (`src/scene/skyAlive.ts`, `readAliveTuning`): `?alive=1&gust=<amplitude sum 0–0.95>&lift=<0–0.5>&shimmer=<0–1>&breath=<0–3>`.
Each is clamped; a missing, empty or unparseable knob is the default (`Number('')` is 0, so an empty knob must read
as missing); the gust clamp keeps the warped clock monotone (T12 proves it at the maximum). The knobs flow through
uniforms in the layout effect, so a change never rebuilds the geometry. Mark dials the strength on the preview;
the values that read right become the defaults.
